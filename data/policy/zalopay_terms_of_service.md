## I. Legal Basis

This agreement is based on:
- Anti-Money Laundering Law
- Consumer Rights Protection Law
- Decree 52/2024/ND-CP on non-cash payment
- Circular 40/2024/TT-NHNN on payment intermediary services
- License No. 22/GP-NHNN issued to Zion

---

## II. Definitions

- **Zalopay:** An intermediate payment service product deployed via the app or Zalo
- **Zalopay Account:** An account provided by Zion to use its services
- **Zalopay E-Wallet:** A payment instrument deployed by Zion; customers top up and conduct transactions
- **Available Balance:** The amount available for use excluding frozen/locked amounts
- **Zalopay Wallet Limit:** The spending limit set by Zion's regulations

---

## III. Account Opening and Usage

### Account Opening Requirements

- Provide complete required information and documents
- Complete identity verification through Zion's KYC process
- Provide additional information depending on the product/service type

### Registration Methods

- Direct registration on Zalopay
- Login via Zalo account (with authorization to share information)

### Account Suspension Cases

- At the customer's request
- Detection of violations/suspected fraud
- No activity for **30 consecutive business days**
- Identity information changed without updating
- Request from a competent authority

---

## IV. Using the Zalopay Wallet

### Customer Rights

- Use Zalopay wallet and features
- Have Zion guarantee the safety of the balance
- Receive transaction information
- File complaints regarding errors

### Customer Responsibilities

- Comply with the Regulations
- Provide complete, accurate, and timely information
- Do not open anonymous, fake wallets, or open wallets on behalf of others
- Take responsibility for transactions
- Fulfill tax obligations

### Zion's Rights

- Set conditions of use
- Set types and levels of fees
- Select partner banks
- Automatic debt deduction as per Regulations
- Refuse/suspend service upon detection of violations

---

## V. Transaction Limits

- **Total individual limit:** Maximum **100,000,000 VND/month**
- Exceptions: Payment service merchants contracted with Zion, public transactions, bill payments

---

## VI. Wallet Freezing and Closure

### Wallet Freezing

Applied when:
- Processing fraud/mistakes
- Suspected legal violations
- Request from a competent authority

### Wallet Closure

Applied when:
- Violations (fraud, impersonation)
- No activity for **6 consecutive months**
- Customer death/disappearance/incapacity
- Request from a competent authority

---

## VII. Account Security

**Security requirements:**
- Protect login credentials, digital signatures, and passwords
- Change passwords periodically or as required
- Do not share authentication information
- Do not rent/lend the account
- Protect devices from viruses/malware
- Do not use rooted/jailbroken devices
- Update to the latest version of Zalopay

---

## VIII. Dispute and Complaint Handling

### Filing Deadlines

- **General:** 60 days from when the activity occurred
- **Zalopay wallet/international cards:** 90 days
- **Domestic cards:** 60 days

### Processing Timeframes

- **From Zalopay wallet:** 10 business days
- **From international cards:** 10 business days
- **From domestic cards/accounts:** 6 business days
- **Maximum:** 30 business days
