## 1. Introduction

Shopee is committed to protecting personal data in accordance with Vietnamese law. This policy explains how personal information of users (buyers and sellers) is collected, used, and processed.

---

## 2. When Data Is Collected

Shopee collects information when users:
- Register an account
- Submit forms
- Conduct transactions
- Communicate through various channels
- Use the application
- Share device location
- Link social media accounts

---

## 3. Types of Data Collected

Includes:
- Full name, email, date of birth, address
- Banking information
- Phone number
- Images/videos
- Device information
- Location data
- Transaction history

---

## 4. Automatically Collected Data

The system records:
- IP address
- Operating system
- Browser type

Shopee uses cookies to track user activity on the platform.

---

## 5. Cookies

Shopee uses cookies to improve services and provide relevant content. Users can disable cookies through browser settings.

---

## 6. Use of Information

Data is used to:
- Process transactions
- Manage accounts
- Improve services
- Marketing
- Credit assessment
- Legal compliance
- Fraud prevention
- Data backup

---

## 7. Information Protection

Shopee implements security measures such as encryption and restricted employee access. However, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 8. Disclosure of Information

Data may be shared with:

The service is not intended for children under 13. Shopee will delete data if discovered to originate from children.
