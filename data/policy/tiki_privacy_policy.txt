## 1. Consent

By providing personal information to Tiki, users agree to the privacy terms. The company reserves the right to modify this policy at any time and encourages customers to regularly review updates.

---

## 2. Purposes of Collection

Personal information is collected for:

- **Orders:** Processing issues related to orders
- **Account Maintenance:** Creating and maintaining accounts, including loyalty programs
- **Consumer Services:** Responding to customer complaints and requests
- **Personalization:** Improving the experience, tailoring services to needs, providing targeted advertising
- **Security:** Preventing account destruction or impersonation activities
- **Legal Requirements:** Collection as required by applicable law

---

## 3. Scope of Collection

Information is collected when:

- Users directly provide it (full name, email, phone, address, login credentials)
- Customers interact on the platform (using cookies and tracking technologies)
- From other legitimate sources

---

## 4. Retention Period

Personal information is stored until the customer requests deletion or logs in and performs the deletion themselves. In all cases, data is secured on Tiki's servers.

---

## 5. No Sharing of Personal Information

Tiki does not provide personal information to third parties, except:

- Partners providing services related to order fulfillment (limited to necessary information)
- Third-party service providers (must comply with personal data protection laws)
- Affiliate, co-execution, and outsourcing programs
- Legal requirements when necessary
- Business transfers (in the case of mergers or acquisitions)

---

## 6. Data Security

The company implements multiple security measures:

- Storing information in a secure operating environment
- Only employees, representatives, and service providers with need-to-know access
- Complying with industry standards and regulations in security
- Notifying authorities and customers if servers are attacked
- Securing payment information according to industry standards

---

## 7. Customer Rights

**Basic rights:**
- Provide information and change decisions at any time
- Self-check, update, and adjust personal information

**Withdrawal/Deletion:**
Customers can delete/close their Tiki account; the system will delete all personal data.

**Information Adjustment:**
Go to Tiki Account Information and perform Adjustments.

**Data Access/Correction Requests:**
- Registered users: Access through the Account Settings page
- Non-registered: Submit a written request to the Data Management staff at https://tiki.vn/lien-he/gui-yeu-cau
