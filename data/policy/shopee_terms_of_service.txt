Source: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/4/article/77243
Company: Shopee Vietnam Co., Ltd.

---

## 1. Introduction & Scope

Shopee operates as an e-commerce marketplace connecting buyers and sellers. "The actual purchase agreement is directly between the Buyer and Seller" — Shopee acts as an intermediary managing transactions.

---

## 2. Privacy & Data

Users authorize Shopee to collect and process personal information in accordance with the Privacy Policy. Sellers who receive other users' data must comply with data protection laws and allow data deletion requests.

---

## 3. Account & Security

- Users must maintain password confidentiality
- Shopee reserves the right to delete accounts for violations
- Sub-accounts are available for sellers with shared responsibility

---

## 4. Prohibited Activities

Users may not:
- Post illegal content
- Engage in fraud
- Use automated tools without permission
- Violate intellectual property rights

---

## 5. Payment & Transactions

- Multiple payment methods supported (cards, COD, bank transfer, e-wallets)
- Shopee Guarantee system holds payments until transaction completion
- Shopee Xu rewards program has expiration dates

---

## 6. Fees

- Transaction processing fee: **6%**
- Fixed commission varies by product category
- Service fees for specific transactions

---

## 7. Dispute Resolution

Buyers and sellers should resolve issues directly first; Shopee provides support for eligible transactions.

---

## 8. Liability Limitations

- Services provided "as-is" without warranties
