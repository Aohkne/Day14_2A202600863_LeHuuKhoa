## A. General Provisions

### Definitions

Over 40 terms are defined including:
- Payment services (QR codes, POS, PhonePOS)
- Tax filing services (invoices, electronic documents)
- Transaction types (valid, fraudulent, refund, chargeback)
- Card categories (domestic vs. international)

### Common Commitments

All parties are required to:
- Provide accurate information
- Implement information security and fraud prevention
- Cooperate on risk management and investigations

### VNPAY Rights

- Suspend service for violations or legal compliance
- Collect fees as agreed
- Protect intellectual property for all developed systems

### Merchant Obligations

- Operate legally and with proper authorization
- Not charge customers extra fees for card payments
- Retain transaction documentation for **18 months**
- Comply with regulatory standards

## B. Transaction Processing & Payment Services

### Refund Policy

- Percentage-based refund of service fees
- No refund of fixed fees for partial refunds
- Exclusions for deposits and software maintenance

### Dispute Resolution

- Response timeframe: **3-5 business days**
- Document submission required within **3 days**
- Transaction verification procedures

### Equipment Management

- POS machine care and security protocols
- Replacement procedures (3 business days)
- Damage compensation calculations based on depreciation
- Return obligation within **5 business days** of notice

## C. MOTO Transactions

Detailed requirements for mail order/telephone order payments:

## D. Installment Conversion Support

VNPAY assists customers in converting purchases to 0% interest installments through partner banks.

**Fee schedule by bank:** 1.8% – 8.9% depending on the installment period.

## F. Contract Duration & Termination

- **Term:** 3 years, auto-renewing annually unless 30 days' notice given
- **Early Termination:** 15 business days' notice for material breach; 30 days for convenience
- **Post-Termination Obligations:** Financial settlements and dispute resolution continue
